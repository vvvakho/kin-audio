# 6 Oct 2026

## Intent

I decided to treat Kin as an open research project rather than attach another general music model to the plugin. The contract is content-preserving re-instrumentation: I author the notes, timing, dynamics, and phrasing; the model changes the performing body.

## Landed

`vvvakho/kin-audio` is public under Apache-2.0. It contains a deterministic paired-corpus generator, monophonic pitch and loudness extraction, a compact DDSP-style harmonic renderer, reproducible CPU/CUDA environments, training and inference commands, structural evaluation, tests, CI, and a dataset registry.

The first end-to-end smoke model trained from generated CC0 pairs and completed inference. On that controlled corpus it measured 2.1 cents median pitch error, 97.4% voicing overlap, and 75.5% of jointly voiced frames within 50 cents. These numbers validate the pipeline, not musical quality or real-recording generalization.

A read-only Gradio dashboard runs on the home PC and is reachable from my phone over the private Tailscale network. It shows project state, approved datasets, run metrics, and model-versus-target listening artifacts.

## Decisions

GitHub holds code, review, CI, and engineering history. Hugging Face will hold public datasets, model cards, weights, and eventually the demo. Cloud NVIDIA is the canonical full-training environment; the RX 7800 XT remains useful for preprocessing, inference, and short experiments, but Arch is not an officially supported ROCm host.

Public data must have explicit provenance and compatible redistribution terms. Ableton Packs can inform private experiments but will not enter released datasets or weights without written permission. StarNet is the first paired real-audio benchmark; NSynth and Slakh are supporting corpora. Splits are made by composition rather than random audio chunks.

The first intended musical release stays narrow: monophonic voice, guitar, or synth into one compelling choir-like learned body. Polyphonic transfer and drum-to-bass generation remain separate research contracts.

## Current state

The public repository and CI are healthy. The live checkpoint is only a synthetic smoke artifact and is intentionally not published as a model release. The next credible evidence must come from a leakage-safe StarNet subset and real source recordings. Local storage is too small for full StarNet or Slakh extraction, so real-data work needs streamed subsets, added storage, or cloud object storage.
